# Engineering Principles

## What this covers

The beliefs and the decision method that everything else in this handbook derives
from: the principles we hold to when they conflict with the expedient thing, and the
framework we run significant decisions through before committing to them.

## Why it matters

Standards tell you what to do; principles tell you what to do when there is no standard
yet, or when two standards collide. Without a shared _why_, every edge case becomes a
debate and every reviewer argues from a different place.

## How to use this section

Read the principles once, so you recognise them when they show up in review comments.
Reach for the [Decision Framework](../decision-framework.md) whenever you are about to
build, buy, or change something significant — it is the ten questions every non-trivial
decision should survive.
